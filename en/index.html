Privacy policy

Offline game. No ads or real-money purchases. Progress stays on your device.

Updated November 23, 2026

1. App and developer contact

This policy covers the Android game Solar Explorer 3D (package org.solarexplorer.prototype) and this privacy policy website. Contact the developer about the game or personal data at suhanovdmitry@gmail.com.

The game has no required account, advertising, real-money purchases or integrated analytics. Money, fuel and spacecraft purchases are game mechanics, not real financial transactions.

2. Data used on your device

The game stores research and landing achievements, soil delivery counts, your virtual balance, fuel, cargo, available and selected spacecraft, interface language, music preference and the technical save-format version on your device.

The system language is used to choose the initial interface language. The game processes button presses, touch input, window, graphics and audio settings locally as needed for controls and rendering. This information is not sent to the developer.

The app does not request a player’s name, email, date of birth or payment details, and does not access contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, advertising IDs or persistent device identifiers.

3. Use and sharing of game data

Local data allows you to continue your expedition and retain preferences. The game works offline and does not send progress, preferences or identifiers to a developer server, advertising service or analytics service. The developer does not receive or sell this game data.

The reviewed Android release 1.0 (21) requests no Android permissions, including internet access. It uses the Godot engine and standard AndroidX and Kotlin libraries; no advertising, payment or analytics SDKs were found.

4. Save retention and deletion

Progress is stored in the app’s private storage in expedition.json; a temporary file may be created while saving. It persists between sessions until app data is deleted. The game has no cloud saves and disables standard Android backup. System transfers or backups outside the app depend on the device and operating-system settings.

To delete all local saves and preferences, use “Clear storage/data” in Android’s app settings or uninstall the app. “New expedition” resets game progress but is not a substitute for clearing all app data. The developer cannot remotely access the save on your device.

5. Opening this page

The privacy policy link in the next game update opens a separate browser only when you press it, without adding progress or identifiers to the page address. The already released version 1.0 (21) does not yet have this button.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process your IP address, browser information and technical request logs to provide and protect the hosting service. This is separate from the game’s local data. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not add analytics, forms, JavaScript trackers, external fonts, CDNs or our own cookies to this site. Your browser and the hosting service are also governed by their own policies.

6. Support requests

If you voluntarily email support, the developer receives your sender address, message and any files you attach. This information is used to respond and investigate the issue, and is retained as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

The support mailbox uses Gmail; email providers process correspondence under their own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy. The game does not automatically send emails, saves or diagnostic reports.

7. Android and app stores

Android, Google Play and RuStore may separately process installation, update, device-operation and crash information under their own policies and your settings. The game has no separate service for sending analytics or error reports to the developer. Android or the engine may produce system diagnostic logs.

8. Your requests and rights

For questions about data handling, or to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support, email suhanovdmitry@gmail.com. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law. To delete local game data, follow section 4.

The game has no registration, personal profiles or date-of-birth collection. We do not request children’s personal information through the game. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or legal guardian may contact the address above.

9. Policy changes

If game features or data handling change, we will update this page and the date below. The current policy is available at this address. Last updated: November 23, 2026.
